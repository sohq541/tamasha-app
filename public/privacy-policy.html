Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YouSeries ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website or app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, username, and password (stored in encrypted/hashed form, never in plain text)
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, and profile photo (provided by Google)
  • Profile information: bio, website link, and profile photo (that you choose to add)
  • Content you upload: videos, photos, titles, and descriptions
  • Activity data: likes, comments, views, and follows
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, and device information (used for security and troubleshooting)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and to let you log in
  • To show and recommend content to you
  • To power features like comments, likes, and follows
  • For security and fraud prevention
  • To improve the website (fixing bugs, building new features)

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use a secure login cookie to keep you signed in. Some preferences (such as liked or viewed videos) are saved in your browser's local storage — this data stays on your device only and is not sent to our servers.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Uploaded videos and photos are stored with a third-party cloud storage provider (Backblaze B2). Account and activity data is stored in our application database.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Information is only shared when:

  • You choose to post public profile information or content (visible to other users)
  • It is required by law (a legal request)
  • It is necessary for our cloud storage or hosting providers to operate the service

6. Your Rights

  • You can edit your profile information at any time
  • You can delete your content or account
  • You can contact us to request correction or deletion of your data

7. Children's Privacy

This service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a significant change, the "Last updated" date on this page will be updated.
